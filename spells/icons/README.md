# Spell icons

Gesture diagrams (white stroke, round start dot, arrowhead at the end) by
**eigger**, from
[eigger/hass-magic-caster-wand `docs/gestures`](https://github.com/eigger/hass-magic-caster-wand/tree/master/docs/gestures),
used unmodified under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.

OpenCaster shows them on spell cards.  Recognition uses the SVG templates in
`spells/`; a spell without an icon here is drawn from its SVG instead.
Icons are matched to spells by name, ignoring case, spaces and punctuation
(`wingardium_leviosa.png` → `Wingardium Leviosa.svg`).
