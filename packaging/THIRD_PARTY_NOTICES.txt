Third-party components bundled with OpenCaster for Windows
==========================================================

OpenCaster.exe is built with PyInstaller and includes the components
below, unmodified.  Each remains under its own licence.

    Licence: GNU Lesser General Public License v3 (LGPL-3.0)
    https://www.qt.io/qt-for-python  -  source: https://code.qt.io/
    Qt and PySide6 are dynamically linked: their DLLs and .pyd files are in
    _internal\PySide6 and can be replaced with compatible builds.

    https://ffmpeg.org/
